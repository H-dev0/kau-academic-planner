About the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Accounting program has been a cornerstone of the Faculty of Economics and Administration at King Abdulaziz University since the Department's establishment in 1972 (1392H). The program offers an advanced curriculum that integrates theoretical frameworks with modern professional practices, complying with national and international accreditation standards. It aims to prepare national competencies equipped with advanced analytical and technical skills and strong ethical values, capable of competing in a dynamic labor market. Aligned with the College's strategy, the program is committed to fostering institutional sustainability and directing its educational output to serve national priorities and realize the goals of Saudi Vision 2030.

Vision & Mission

Vision

A leading program in accounting education and practice, effectively contributing to building a knowledge society and achieving sustainable development through academic excellence and active community partnership

Mission

To cultivate a high-level quality accounting program and learning environment that equip graduates with the knowledge and skills of present-day accounting and business. In turn enabling graduates to meet the demands of the constantly evolving labor market and to positively contribute to society.

Program Goals

  • 1Deliver current accounting knowledge through an updated curriculum: Maintain a curriculum that continually integrates the latest accounting knowledge.
  • 2Enhance practical skills: Ensure that graduates have ample opportunities for practical training and hands-on experience in their field.
  • 3Promote social responsibility: Encourage student involvement in community service and initiatives that positively contribute to society.
  • 4Prepare qualified graduates: Preparing students to obtain professional certificates.
  • 5Facilitate internships and networking: Provide students with internship and networking opportunities, helping them build valuable connections and real-world experience.

Learning Outcomes

  • Demonstrate advance accounting knowledge related to the theories and current practices.
  • Demonstrate business knowledge related to the theories and current practices that are essential for accounting graduates.
  • Identify, analyse, and solve business and accounting issues, utilizing the required accounting techniques and methods.
  • Apply professional, communication and interpersonal skills that make competitive accounting graduates.
  • Apply digital, numerical, and research skills to solve business and accounting problems.
  • Perform accounting tasks in compliance with accounting standards, regulations, and codes of ethics.
  • Demonstrate leadership and teamworking skills and values.

Graduate Attributes

Cognitive and Technical Proficiency: Possess comprehensive knowledge of accounting principles and financial reporting standards, with the ability to utilize modern accounting technologies.Analytical Thinking and Problem Solving: Ability to analyze complex business and accounting issues and provide innovative solutions to support decision-making.Ethical and Professional Commitment: Adhere to the highest standards of integrity, professional conduct, and social responsibility in all accounting practices.Effective Communication: Demonstrate exceptional oral and written communication skills suitable for diverse business environments.Contribution to Sustainability: Awareness of the role of accounting in supporting sustainable development and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) practices.

Study Plans & Courses

Graduation requirements stipulate the completion of the entire study plan and the successful passing of all courses, totaling 125 credit hours.
